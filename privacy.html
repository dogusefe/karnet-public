Kârnet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Türkçe

Kârnet is a Chrome extension developed by Phosfe Software that calculates net profit on product pages for marketplace sellers. This document explains what data the extension accesses and what it does with it.

Summary

Kârnet does not collect any data, does not send any data to a server and does not share any data with third parties. All data is stored only in your own browser.

Data the extension accesses

Product page content. When you open a product page on a supported marketplace (Trendyol, Hepsiburada, n11, Pazarama, Çiçeksepeti, PttAVM, Amazon.com.tr), the extension reads the product name, price, category and brand from that page. This information is used only to fill in the calculator panel.

Values you enter. Product cost, commission rate, shipping cost, service fee and other settings.

The extension does not access your account details, orders, payment details, passwords or personal information on these sites. It does not run on pages outside the supported sites.

Storage

The values you enter, the date the extension was installed, and the name, category and page address of products for which you saved a cost are stored in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). This data does not leave your device and is not synced to your other devices.

Data transfer

The extension makes no network requests. No data is sent to us or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code. There is no account or sign-in.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete a single product's record from the “Products” tab of the settings page.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all stored data.
  • You can export your data to a file from the “Backup” tab of the settings page; the file is saved only on your own computer.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep your settings and product costs in your browser.
  • scripting and site access: to show the calculator panel on product pages of the supported marketplaces and to refresh the panel in open tabs when the extension is updated.

Changes

If a feature that transfers data is added (for example a seller account connection), this policy will be updated beforehand and the feature will not be enabled without your explicit consent.
